\section{Complete Prediction Catalog}\label{app:catalog}

This appendix presents the complete catalog of all 73 predictions tested in the SS-PEG program. The predictions are organized by category: (1) coupling constants (6), (2) mass spectrum (12), (3) CKM mixing (24), (4) PMNS mixing (28), and (5) neutrino mass spectrum (3). Each entry includes the formula, graph value, experimental value, and error. Experimental data is sourced from PDG 2024~\cite{pdg2024} and NuFIT 5.2~\cite{nufit52}.
